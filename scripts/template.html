Method

Cost components

  • Maintenance — subcontractor invoices by invoice date. Invoices repeated on several assets of one work order are split evenly. Registration/tax invoices are removed (calculated below instead). Non-fleet equipment is excluded.
  • Fuel card — WEX net cost (after discounts, including DEF) by unit and transaction month. Generic cards with no unit are charged to the billed store.
  • Onsite diesel — tank gallons × site price at 353 Dayton, 356 Midland and 359 Burnet, split across the site's trucks by that month's miles.
  • Propane — monthly gallons × plan rate by location, split across that location's forklifts by hours. TMFLs carry no propane.
  • Depreciation — straight line from original cost: trucks, LD vehicles and trailers 60 months; TMFLs, forklifts and ancillary 84 months. Starts in the origination month and stops when fully depreciated. Leased units have none. Disposed units use their March 2026 book depreciation.
  • Registration & taxes — taxes 0.0667% of original cost per month (all classes); registration 0.05% per month (trucks, LD vehicles, trailers). Charged in active months, including after an asset is fully depreciated.

Measures

  • All-in cost per mile = all fleet costs at the selected locations ÷ truck & LD telematics miles.
  • Truck & LD cost per mile = costs of trucks and LD vehicles only ÷ their miles.
  • Cost per hour = shown separately for forklifts and TMFLs: each class's costs ÷ its own hours. Hours Jan–Aug are estimates (lifetime average); DVIR actuals start 8/24.
  • Cost per asset-month = trailer / ancillary cost ÷ active asset-months.
  • Run rate = average month in the selected range × 12.

Location & activity

  • Units on the 10/31 roster stay at their roster location all year and are active from their origination month.
  • Disposed units keep their history at their last telematics store (else fuel card store, work-order store, hours location or book cost center) and are active from their first to last month of activity.
